# SEC Regulatory Contract

## 1.0.0 - Phase 0 baseline

- Frozen inventory for local 13F, N-CEN, N-PORT, and RR1 packages.
- Closed source-table and source-column mapping with immutable raw semantics.
- Capability-state and family fixture contracts.
- Existing classification, coverage, and source-completeness gates.
- Recaptured 135 original SEC packages after N-PORT 2023Q4/2025Q1 and N-CEN 2023Q4/2024Q1 became available locally; N-CEN 2024Q1 preserves two SEC-published conditional absences.

Released contract versions are immutable. Any source schema, gate, or fixture
change requires a new reviewed contract version and bundle digest.
